What the fee covers

Everything below is included. If it is not on this list, we do not charge for it.

Printed notes

One set per topic, handed out at the start of the chapter, with the previous year's questions at the back.

A short test every Saturday and a full paper at the end of each month, corrected by hand.

Parent updates

Marks on WhatsApp the same evening, and a longer note once a month on attendance and weak topics.

Saturday doubt hour

Open to every batch, no extra fee, no appointment needed. Come with the chapter you are stuck on.
