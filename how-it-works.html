Running costs

What it costs to keep it online.

Nothing hidden after launch. These are the only ongoing costs, every account is opened in your name, and you pay the provider directly — not me.

  • Domain name (.in or .com)Your address on the internet. Renews every year.
    ₹500 – ₹1,500 a year
  • HostingWhere the site lives. Free if we use a modern free platform — which suits most small sites.
    ₹0 – ₹3,000 a year
  • SSL / the padlockSo the site shows as secure and Google doesn't warn visitors.
    Free
  • Business email on your domainOptional — you can keep using your existing email instead.
    ₹0 – ₹900 a year
  • Payment gatewayOnly if you take payments online. Razorpay/PayU take a small cut per transaction; no monthly fee and no setup cost.
    ~2% per payment

In practice that's about ₹2,000 – ₹4,500 a year to keep a typical site running. My build fee is separate and one-off, and the payment gateway's fee is about 2.36% of each payment — 2% plus GST — deducted by the gateway from what you receive. That comes out of the sale, not out of your build fee.
