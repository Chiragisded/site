Package 1 — from ₹12,000

Kaya Salon & Spa, Vashi

A three-page neighbourhood salon site built to make prices, timings and booking obvious before a customer has to call.

Open sample

What this fixes

Customers often call just to ask for prices, hours, or whether Saturday is free. This layout puts the answers up front so WhatsApp messages start with intent, not basic questions.

What the site handles

  • Home, full price list, and visit page with hours, address and photos.
  • WhatsApp booking buttons with the message already typed.
  • Mobile-first reading, clear service/pricing scan, and fast static pages.

What you’d provide

  • Service list, prices, and any packages or booking rules.
  • Address, opening hours, WhatsApp number, and Google Maps link.
  • Your own photos, logo/name, and the tone you want the salon to have.

Package 2 — from ₹35,000

Bright Minds Classes, Nerul

A coaching institute site that turns batch interest into a saved enquiry, instead of leaving parents to chase details over calls and DMs.

Open sample

What this fixes

Parents want batch times, fees and the next step in one place. Staff need enquiries captured neatly, not buried in messages. The site makes the offer clear and the enquiry trackable.

What the site handles

  • Home, batches/fees page, and a dedicated booking/enquiry page.
  • A working enquiry form; in this demo, submissions are saved, listed for staff, and downloadable as CSV.
  • Clear fee/batch presentation so parents can compare before contacting you.

What you’d provide

  • Subjects, batch timings, fees, and admission/seat details.
  • Faculty or institute details, location, and contact number.
  • The questions you want every enquiry to answer before a callback.

Package 3 — from ₹55,000

Iron Yard Fitness, Kharghar

A gym site that compares plans clearly and walks a new member through an online joining flow, with the payment step shown as a demo.

Open sample

What this fixes

Gyms lose people when plans are confusing or joining feels like a conversation that has to happen later. This makes the offer comparable and the next step immediate.

What the site handles

  • Home, memberships page, and a gym/details page with hours and location.
  • Plan comparison with a checkout demo: pick a plan, enter details, get a member ID/receipt.
  • In a live build, the payment step runs through Razorpay into your account.

What you’d provide

  • Plan names, prices, inclusions, freeze/cancel rules, and offers.
  • Gym photos, timings, address, trainers/facilities you want highlighted.
  • Your Razorpay account details and the member info you need at sign-up.

Package 4 — premium / brand site

Sable & Stone, Bandra West

A premium studio site where the visual craft carries the brand, while the work, objects and studio story stay easy to explore.

Open sample

What this fixes

High-end brands lose their edge when their website feels generic. This sample shows how a site can feel distinctive without making projects, services, or contact hard to find.
