The studio

Two people, a site diary, and a phone that gets answered.

Founded in 2016 above a bakery in Bandra West. Four projects a year, one point of contact, no renderings we cannot build.

01 — The practice

We started because a wall was built in the wrong place.

Aditi was an architect on a 40-floor tower. Rehan was fitting out flats for other people's architects. One project's shell and interior drawings disagreed by 200 mm — nobody noticed until the kitchen arrived.

So they rented a room, bought one plotter, and started taking jobs small enough that the person drawing could also stand on site. Nine projects later, that is still the arrangement.

We do not render what we cannot build. If a detail costs more than it is worth, we say it in the meeting rather than in the invoice.

02 — Nine years

The short version.

A few dates, in order.

  1. 2016

    One room above a bakery

    A 42 m² studio flat in Khar for a friend who paid in instalments. Still the project we show first.

  2. 2018

    First house, first monsoon

    The Alibaug plot taught us what a rain court is for. Two walls were rebuilt that June. We design for water before furniture now.

  3. 2021

    Two people, four projects

    We stopped taking more than four a year. The studio got quieter, the work got better, and the fee stayed put for two years.

  4. 2023

    The site diary starts

    Photos, measurements, the name of whoever promised what — sent to the client every Friday. It ended most of our arguments.

  5. 2026

    Where we are

    Nine built, two on site. One workshop in Vasai that makes most of our joinery.

05 — For whoever maintains this

How this page is built.

No framework, no build step — plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so it loads fast on a phone in a lift. Everything degrades to readable text if a script fails.

The light behind the headline
A WebGL shader written by hand in GLSL. Low frame rate, pauses off screen, and shows a still gradient where WebGL is missing or motion is reduced.
Rooms that turn
CSS 3D transforms driven by one scroll value. Three divs deep, no 3D engine, nothing to download.
Motion + anime.js
Section reveals and staggered entrances run through Motion. anime.js splits the headline into characters and words.
Component patterns
Spotlight cards, magnetic buttons, the marquee, tilt cards and the timeline beam are adapted from Magic UI, KokonutUI, React Bits and Bklit — rewritten as plain CSS and JS.
Not here yet
Rive needs an authored .riv file exported from the Rive editor, so this page does not pretend to use it. Add one and the same layout holds an interactive diagram.
