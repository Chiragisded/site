01 — The studio

A building is the outside of a room. We draw them as one thing.

Most projects split in two: an architect does the shell, someone else fills it. That handover is where good ideas get lost.

We kept it in one room. Aditi draws the structure and the light. Rehan draws the joinery, the kitchen, the way a door sits in a wall. Every week one of us stands on site, and the drawings change the same afternoon.

We take four projects a year. That is how many site visits two people can do.

04 — How we work

Small studio. Short chain of command.

The person who draws a detail checks it on site and takes your call about it.

01

One drawing, both halves

Structure, joinery, light and furniture live in one file — nothing lost between architect and interior team, because there is no other team.

02
1:5

Details you can touch

Shadow gaps, switch heights, the round on a stair nosing. Drawn at 1:5, handed to one carpenter with one number.

03

A site visit every week

Same day, same time, photographed and written up. You get the note that evening.

04

Materials from close by

Laterite from Sindhudurg, terrazzo from Vasai, teak from a yard we have used nine years.

05

One fee, after sign-off

We quote when the drawings are final. Changes are billed hourly, at a rate stated up front.

“The only thing that changed after sign-off was the client's mind about a doorknob.”
