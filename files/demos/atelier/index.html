01 — The studio

A building is the outside of a room. We draw them as one thing.

Most projects split in two: an architect does the shell, someone else fills it. That handover is where the good ideas leak out.

So we kept it in one room. Aditi draws the structure and the light; Rehan draws the joinery, the kitchen, the way a door sits in a wall. Every week one of us stands on site, tablet in hand, and the drawings change the same afternoon.

We take four projects a year — not scarcity marketing, it is how many site visits two people can physically do.

03 — How we work

Small studio. Short chain of command.

No account managers, no hand-off pack. The person who draws a detail checks it on site, and takes your call about it.
